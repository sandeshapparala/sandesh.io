**Preferred result wording once measured:** "During [date range], the system handled [defined count] and recorded [defined outcome]." Replace the bracketed fields only in the later evidence-backed version; do not publish them as placeholders.

## Shared evidence pack

Collect the following for each client:

1. Approved client identity, project name, and permission for the proposed public assets.
2. A short description of the original problem in the client's own words.
3. Sandesh's role, delivered scope, and approximate delivery period.
4. One anonymised conversation matched to a lead or action record.
5. One useful dashboard or workflow screenshot.
6. Measurement definitions, dates, sample size, and source records for any results claimed.
7. A genuine attributable testimonial, if available; preserve its approved meaning.
8. Confirmation of which features and maintenance arrangements are current.

### Response-time evidence

Define whether timing runs from the customer's send action or server receipt, and whether it ends at delivery or provider acceptance. Measure the first useful response separately from an acknowledgement. Report the sample period, number of measured interactions, median, and slower-end experience where available. Do not equate model execution time with WhatsApp delivery time.

Choose the first detailed case study based on the strongest evidence. Yutha is the proposed lead story, subject to that check. Use concise summaries for the remaining projects until their deeper material is ready. Clearly distinguish client implementations, demonstrations, measured results, and future scope options.
