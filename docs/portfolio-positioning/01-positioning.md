## Best-fit customer

Start with developers and construction businesses that receive regular enquiries and have a sales or consultation team responsible for following them up. Prioritise businesses whose questions, qualification criteria, and next steps can be described clearly.

Likely buyers are owners, sales heads, and marketing heads. Good buying triggers include a new project launch, increasing inbound volume, repeated delays in replying, inconsistent qualification, or poor visibility into conversations.

Volume alone is not the qualification rule. Consider the value of the workflow, complexity, willingness to provide accurate information, and the team's ability to act on qualified enquiries.

Use direct access to the engineer, client-specific workflows, full implementation, and ongoing improvement as the reasons to choose Sandesh. Do not claim these capabilities are exclusive or technically unique.

## Voice and messaging rules

- Use first-person language: "I build", "I review", and "we agree" when describing collaboration with the client.
- Explain the business workflow before tools or model names.
- Show your face in the introduction, walkthrough, and About page. Keep the same name and professional title across channels.
- Use "agent" for a system that can take the actions being described. Explain those actions rather than relying on the label.
- Treat speed as one performance measure alongside answer quality, booking reliability, and useful handoffs.
- Keep web development visible as supporting engineering experience.
- Lead with the real-estate offer; give other buyers an explicit route to custom automation.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives, guaranteed sales, invented percentages, or claims of full autonomy.

## Commercial measurement

Track source → service or case-study visit → demo engagement → consultation request → qualified consultation → proposal → implementation → monthly management.

Judge positioning by qualified opportunities, fit, wins, and retained monthly revenue. Page views and demo clicks are supporting signals. Begin with referrals, property-marketing partners, and short personal walkthroughs that link to relevant client stories.
