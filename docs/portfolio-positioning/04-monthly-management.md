# Asset 4 — Monthly AI agent management

## Public copy

**Offer name:** AI Agent Management & Improvement

**Headline:** Keep your agent useful as your business changes.

Project information changes. Customers ask new questions. Your team's process evolves. I provide ongoing management for the agents I build, with a defined schedule for reviewing conversations, updating knowledge, investigating issues, and improving the agreed workflow.

Each month, you receive a clear account of what happened, what changed, and what needs attention next.

### Included in a scoped plan

- Regular review of a defined conversation sample.
- Updates to approved project or service information.
- Investigation of reported issues within the agreed support scope.
- A defined allowance for small fixes and workflow improvements.
- A monthly report and review conversation.

Implementation and monthly management are quoted separately. The proposal identifies included work, expected volume, support arrangements, and any third-party charges.

**CTA:** Discuss Ongoing Agent Management

## Recommended base scope for future proposals

This is the default offer to price and adapt, not a statement of existing client contract terms. Use it only after confirming delivery capacity and the installed system's monitoring capabilities.

| Item | Base scope |
| --- | --- |
| System covered | One agreed production agent for one business on one WhatsApp number |
| Workflow covered | The deployed enquiry, qualification, booking/request, and handoff paths explicitly listed in the proposal |
| Initial baseline | Document current behaviour, known issues, ownership, and available measurements before management starts |
| Routine review | Weekly review of available error/delivery indicators and flagged conversations |
| Conversation sampling | Up to 50 conversations per month, including flagged cases and an ordinary sample |
| Change allowance | Four engineering hours per month for approved knowledge updates, small fixes, and tuning; scheduled after prioritisation |
| Report | One monthly report covering activity, reviewed quality, failures, changes, and priorities |
| Review call | One 30-minute monthly call with the client's designated owner |
| Support channel | One agreed email or ticket channel to keep issue tracking complete |
| Support window | Monday–Friday, 10:00–18:00 IST, excluding holidays stated in the proposal |
| Response target | Acknowledge support requests by the next business day; this is not a guaranteed resolution deadline |
| Changes beyond allowance | Estimate and obtain approval before extra work; do not incur unapproved overages |
| Unused allowance | Does not roll over unless the proposal expressly provides otherwise |

The four-hour allowance covers active investigation and engineering changes. Routine sampling, reporting, and the review call are listed deliverables outside that allowance. A major incident or large repair receives an estimate if it cannot be addressed within the remaining allowance. Any implementation warranty agreed separately continues under its own terms.

## What counts as a small change

- Updating approved answers, contact details, project facts, or service information.
- Adjusting a qualification question or an existing handoff rule.
- Improving wording or behaviour within an existing workflow.
- Diagnosing and repairing a scoped integration issue where the work fits the allowance.

Group requests into a prioritised list with estimated effort. Validate changes against representative conversations before release and record what changed.

## Separately scoped work

New agents, business numbers, channels, major integrations, substantial dashboard work, new booking infrastructure, bulk migrations, new language rollouts, and large workflow redesigns need their own estimate. Content production, ad management, lead generation, and the client's sales follow-up are separate services.

The base plan does not include continuous staffed monitoring, guaranteed uptime, guaranteed sales, unlimited engineering, or round-the-clock incident response. Any stronger service commitment must be separately resourced and agreed.

## Client responsibilities and dependencies

The client designates an owner, supplies accurate approved information, approves material changes, and handles human escalations. The proposal records account ownership, required access, usage limits, and who pays each provider. Access and data handling should be limited to the work agreed with the client.

Hosting, AI/model usage, WhatsApp/provider fees, and paid external tools are separate from the management fee. Prefer transparent client billing or itemised pass-through terms. Any automatic follow-up workflow must be separately specified, including its messaging permissions and provider requirements.

## Monthly report structure

| Report item | What to show |
| --- | --- |
| Period and coverage | Dates, conversations available, sample reviewed, and any missing data |
| Activity | Defined enquiries, qualification records, bookings/requests, and handoffs where measured |
| Response performance | Defined timing metric with sample size, if instrumentation supports it |
| Reviewed answer quality | Issues found in the sample and their causes; distinguish sampled quality from all conversations |
| Reliability | Delivery/integration problems, known impact, and unresolved issues |
| Changes shipped | What was updated, why, and how it was checked |
| Work allowance | Engineering time used and remaining or any separately approved work |
| Next priorities | Recommended improvements and client inputs needed |

Do not infer sales, time savings, or cost reductions from message counts alone. Label unavailable data as unavailable. Monitoring gaps that require engineering become explicit setup work or an agreed improvement.

## Commercial structure

Quote a one-time implementation fee, a monthly management fee, and third-party usage separately. If taking over an unfamiliar agent, quote an initial assessment and any remediation before accepting the base plan.

Set the fee from the cost of scheduled reviews, reports, support capacity, four included engineering hours, overhead, and margin. Adjust scope or price for higher volume and more complex systems. Avoid a public price until this baseline is costed against actual delivery capacity.

Use a monthly renewal for the default offer, with payment, cancellation notice, data export, and handover terms explicitly stated in the proposal. Do not rely on restricted account access to retain a client. Renewal should be supported by useful operation and visible improvements.
