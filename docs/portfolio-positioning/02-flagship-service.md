### Built around the conversations your team handles every day

A property buyer asks about availability. A homeowner wants to understand your construction services. Another enquiry is ready for a visit but needs help arranging the next step.

Your agent follows a workflow we define together, using approved information and clear rules for involving your team.

### How the engagement works

**1. Define the workflow.** We identify the questions to answer, information to collect, actions to take, and situations that need a person. We agree what a successful implementation should demonstrate.

**2. Build and connect.** I develop the agent around your approved information and the agreed channels, records, and booking process.

**3. Test with realistic enquiries.** We review representative conversations, incomplete information, booking conflicts, and handoffs before launch.

**4. Launch and support adoption.** I help your team understand how to review enquiries, take over conversations, and request changes.

**5. Review and improve.** With monthly management, I review a defined sample of conversations, maintain the agreed knowledge, investigate issues, and report on the next improvements.

### An implementation fee, followed by a scoped monthly option

Implementation covers the agreed build, testing, launch, and handover. Monthly management covers a defined review and improvement schedule after launch.

I quote these separately so you can see what you are paying to build and what you are paying to maintain. Hosting, messaging, AI usage, and other third-party charges are identified separately in the proposal.

### Common questions

**Can it work with our existing WhatsApp number?**  
I assess your current setup and the available connection options before defining the implementation. Number requirements and any migration steps are included in the scope.

**Will it confirm a site visit automatically?**  
That depends on the booking process we connect. It can either record a request for your team or confirm against an agreed availability source. We make the distinction clear in the customer conversation.

**What happens when the agent cannot answer?**  
We define how it handles missing information and when to involve a person. Unsupported project facts or exceptions should be referred to your team.

**Can it use our CRM or support different languages?**  
I assess the systems and language requirements during scoping. Agreed integrations and languages are tested before they are presented as supported.

**Do you work outside real estate?**  
Yes. Real estate and construction are my primary focus. I also build custom AI workflows for businesses with a clear use case and the information needed to support it.

**How much does it cost and how long does it take?**  
I provide a scoped quote and delivery plan after reviewing your workflow, integrations, expected volume, and operational requirements.
